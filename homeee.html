Welcome to Farm2Market

Farm2Market is a digital platform that connects farmers directly with restaurants, shops and consumers. It helps farmers showcase their agricultural products and enables buyers to discover fresh farm produce. Our goal is to support local farmers and make agricultural products easier to find and purchase.

How Farm2Market Works

Our platform makes it easier for farmers and buyers to discover each other.

1

Farmer Joins

Farmers connect with the Farm2Market platform.

2

Add Products

Farmers showcase their available products, prices and quantities.

3

Buyer Orders

Restaurants, shops and consumers find products and place orders.

4

Product Delivery

The order is arranged for delivery to the buyer.
